# SwipeTune Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

SwipeTune is an open-source, local-first Android music player. This policy explains how SwipeTune accesses and handles information.

## Summary

SwipeTune does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data with the developer or third parties. The app has no advertising, analytics, account system, or developer-operated server. Its core features work locally on your device.

## Information accessed on your device

With your permission, SwipeTune reads audio files and the media metadata Android exposes on your device, including titles, artists, albums, folders, duration, and embedded artwork. This access is used to build and display your music library and to play your music.

SwipeTune stores playlists, favorites, playback history, Review decisions, queue state, and preferences locally in the app's private storage. Audio files are not copied into the app's private storage.

## Playback and notifications

SwipeTune uses an Android media playback foreground service so audio can continue while the app is in the background and so playback controls can appear on the lock screen, notification shade, Bluetooth devices, Android Auto, and compatible accessories.

## Backup and transfer

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may back up SwipeTune's private app data through the backup provider configured on the device. This can include settings, playlists, playback history, and Review decisions, but not your audio files. The developer does not receive or control those backups.

You can also create a portable backup manually. SwipeTune writes that backup only to the location you select through Android's system file picker. You control and are responsible for that file.

## Deleting music

SwipeTune only requests deletion of an audio file after you choose a delete action and confirm it. On supported Android versions, Android displays an additional system confirmation. Deleted files cannot be recovered by SwipeTune.

## Network access

SwipeTune does not request Android's internet permission. Opening the privacy policy or source-code page hands the link to your chosen browser; the browser's privacy practices then apply.

## Children's privacy

SwipeTune is a general-audience utility and is not directed specifically to children. Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected in this document and its effective date. The current version is always available in the public source repository.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the public project contact channel at https://github.com/projectescape/swipetune/issues.
