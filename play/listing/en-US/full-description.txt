SwipeTune is a private, local-first music player built to make large offline libraries feel lively, fluid, and easy to manage.

PLAY YOUR MUSIC
• Automatically indexes music available on your Android device
• Supports MP3, FLAC, M4A, AAC, WAV, OGG, and other device-compatible formats
• Shows album artwork, title, artist, album, genre, and folder information
• Provides a full player, persistent mini player, live queue, search, sorting, and shuffle
• Integrates with the lock screen, notification shade, Bluetooth, headsets, Android Auto, and a home-screen widget

ORGANIZE YOUR LIBRARY
• Browse songs, albums, artists, genres, folders, favorites, and playback history
• Create and manage ordered playlists
• Import and export M3U and M3U8 playlists
• Find likely duplicate recordings while keeping deletion under your control
• Back up playlists, settings, history, and Review decisions

OPTIONAL REVIEW MODE
Review is there when you want it—not on the main screen.

Choose all unreviewed music or one folder, then listen and decide:
• Swipe right to keep
• Swipe left to reject
• Undo the last decision
• Leave and continue the same session later

Rejected songs remain on your device but stay hidden from the normal SwipeTune library. Inspect them in the built-in Rejected playlist, restore them, or explicitly delete them after confirmation.

EXPRESSIVE AND PERSONAL
• Material 3 Expressive interface
• System, light, and dark themes
• Optional Android dynamic color
• Artwork-driven atmosphere, fluid transitions, and animated scrubbers
• Display-cutout-safe edge-to-edge layouts

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
SwipeTune has no ads, analytics, accounts, developer-operated servers, or tracking. Your library and listening metadata stay under your control.
